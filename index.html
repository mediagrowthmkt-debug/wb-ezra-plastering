Repair, restore or replace: how we decide

Not every cracked wall or ceiling needs to be torn out. We start by finding out why the plaster failed, settling, moisture, or a failed past repair, and then give you an honest recommendation in writing: repair what can be saved, restore original plaster where it is worth keeping, and only replace when it genuinely makes sense. You get the reasoning, not just a price.

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Malden and serve Greater Boston, including Newton, Brookline, Chestnut Hill, North Reading and Boston. If your town is nearby, just ask.

Do you really put the price in writing before starting?

Always. We walk the job, explain the right approach, and give you a written, itemized scope with the price agreed up front. No surprise charges at the end.

Is the owner actually on the job?

Yes. We do not hand your home to an anonymous subcontracted crew. The owner is on site from planning through the final walkthrough.

Do you offer finishes like Venetian plaster and microcement?

Yes, and almost no plastering contractor in the area does. We install Venetian plaster, microcement and tadelakt, plus traditional plaster and repair work.

Can you respond quickly to water or ceiling damage?

Yes. Water damage gets worse the longer it waits, so we prioritize it and can often provide a same-week estimate. Call or text the owner directly.
